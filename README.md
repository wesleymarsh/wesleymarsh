### Dungeon Forge
A grid-based procedural dungeon generator for Unreal Engine 5.4.4, designed to be modular and easy for developers with no C++ experience to use through Blueprints.

The initial prototype was developed over the course of 6 weeks, with 4 weeks of research and planning and 2 weeks of development. Dungeon Forge will be open sourced after a refactor to improve performance and make the plugin more intuitive to use.
